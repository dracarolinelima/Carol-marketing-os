## Operating Rules

- Keep only information needed to coordinate current work.
- Do not use this file as a source of permanent strategy, audience truth, 
scientific truth or performance truth.
- Marketing Core has precedence for stable marketing strategy and positioning.
- Scientific claims must be supported by the Scientific Research workflow.
- Internal audience conclusions must be supported by Audience Intelligence.
- External market conclusions must be supported by Market Research.
- Performance conclusions must follow the Performance Analyst source-truth 
rules.
- Do not treat Cliente IO AI classifications as ground truth.
- Preserve required human approval gates.
- Do not store identifiable patient information in this file.
- When evidence is insufficient, mark the task as Waiting for Input, 
Verification Required or Blocked rather than inventing an answer.

Keep this section short. It exists only to preserve immediate continuity between 
handoffs.

Do not automatically transfer every completed task into campaign-history.md.

A completed task should enter a durable memory or history file only when it 
creates information worth retaining, such as:

- a real published content or campaign record;
- a verified downstream performance result;
- a reviewed strategic learning;
- a meaningful human correction;
- a decision that affects future execution.

Routine production steps, temporary drafts and ordinary handoffs should not 
become permanent memory.

## Memory Boundary

- What is permanently true about Carol's positioning?
- What does the audience permanently want?

- What content always works?
- What campaign caused a business outcome?

Those conclusions belong in their designated source-of-truth or reviewed 
learning layer.
