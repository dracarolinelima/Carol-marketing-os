# Email Sequence — Carol Marketing OS Compatibility Guard

## Status

INACTIVE BY DEFAULT.

Email marketing is not part of the current primary Carol Marketing OS workflow.

This skill exists only to prevent legacy routing or Starter logic from 
introducing SaaS-style email funnels into the current system.

---

## Activation Rule

Use this skill only when Carol explicitly asks to:

- create an email sequence;
- develop email marketing;
- build a nurture flow;
- create a welcome sequence;
- design re-engagement emails;
- define an email strategy;
- adapt an approved campaign to email.

Do not invoke automatically because a campaign exists.

Do not invoke automatically for:

- Instagram content;
- Meta Ads;
- CRM follow-up;
- lead qualification;
- patient conversion;
- retention;
- recurrence;
- referral;
- content calendars;
- campaign planning.

---

## Current System Rule

Until Carol explicitly activates email as a channel:

- do not recommend email as a default channel;
- do not create email deliverables automatically;
- do not add email to campaign briefs by default;
- do not create nurture sequences automatically;
- do not create onboarding flows;
- do not create re-engagement flows;
- do not create SaaS-style lifecycle automation;
- do not apply product activation frameworks;
- do not apply free-trial logic;
- do not apply usage-triggered upsell logic;
- do not apply tier-upgrade logic.

---

## If Email Marketing Is Explicitly Activated

Before writing any sequence:

1. identify the actual business objective;
2. identify the audience;
3. identify the relationship stage;
4. identify the patient journey stage;
5. identify what the email should accomplish;
6. confirm that email is an appropriate channel;
7. preserve Marketing Core positioning;
8. preserve scientific and medical boundaries;
9. preserve privacy and professional rules;
10. preserve human approval before external sending.

Do not inherit generic SaaS templates automatically.

---

## Strategy

If the strategic role of email is unclear:

route to:

`MOS-Strategist`

Do not invent:

- sequence length;
- timing;
- funnel stage;
- CTA;
- lead magnet;
- nurture logic;
- reactivation logic.

---

## Medical Content

If an email contains a material medical claim that is not already verified:

route to:

`MOS-Scientific Research`

Do not strengthen evidence for persuasion.

---

## Copy

When strategy is clear:

route to:

`MOS-Creator / Copywriter`

The copy should preserve:

- autoridade tranquila;
- clareza;
- profundidade;
- proximidade;
- natural Brazilian Portuguese;
- scientific proportionality;
- patient dignity.

---

## Editorial Review

Before sending substantive medical or marketing email:

route to:

`MOS-Editor`

when appropriate.

Sending remains subject to human approval.

---

## Privacy

Patient and lead data are sensitive.

Do not expose:

- identifiable patient data;
- medical information;
- contact information;
- CRM data

outside authorized systems.

Do not create segmentation using inappropriate sensitive profiling.

---

## CTA

Do not default to:

- urgency;
- scarcity;
- pressure;
- repeated scheduling prompts;
- aggressive conversion language.

CTA should match the approved strategic role of the email.

---

## Legacy Logic That Must Not Be Used

Do not use legacy Starter frameworks such as:

- Ghost Users
- One-and-Done Users
- Power Users
- Activation Gap
- Free → Self-serve
- Self-serve → Managed
- Managed → Full Service
- usage-based upsell
- SaaS onboarding
- trial activation
- product-tier escalation
- churn prevention logic

unless Carol later creates a separate non-clinical product where those concepts 
genuinely apply.

---

## Output

If Carol explicitly requests an email sequence, use the format most useful for 
the task.

Possible fields:

- objective;
- audience;
- trigger;
- relationship stage;
- sequence logic;
- email timing;
- subject;
- body;
- CTA;
- medical/evidence notes;
- editor flags;
- human approval status.

Do not force a fixed number of emails.

Do not force fixed timing intervals.

---

## Final Principle

Email is a channel, not a mandatory funnel stage.

Carol Marketing OS should use it only when there is a clear strategic reason to 
do so.

Until then, this skill remains inactive by default.
